# AssetRail names and branding

Apache-2.0 does not grant permission to use the AssetRail or RSI Tech trade
names, trademarks, service marks, or product names except for the reasonable
and customary uses described in Section 6. Official logos, official application
icon, `ai.rsitech.assetrail` bundle identity, domains, accounts, and service
identity are not granted for use by modified distributions.

You may make truthful nominative statements such as “based on AssetRail source”
or “an unofficial fork of AssetRail.” A modified distribution must not use
AssetRail as its primary product name, reuse official artwork or bundle
identity, imply endorsement, or direct users to official support as though the
fork were an official release.

Modified distributions should use their own name, artwork, bundle identifier,
Apple team, credentials, support, security, and privacy disclosures. The
default community build demonstrates this separation.

Questions about permitted brand use may be sent to
[info@rsitech.ai](mailto:info@rsitech.ai). This file limits only project identity;
it does not reduce rights granted by Apache-2.0.
