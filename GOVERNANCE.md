# Governance

AssetRail uses a maintainer-led governance model for the `0.1.x` prototype.

## Maintainer authority

`@s1korrrr` is the initial maintainer and has final authority for:

- issue and pull-request triage;
- merge and release decisions;
- security-response coordination;
- Code of Conduct enforcement;
- appointment or removal of maintainers;
- use of the AssetRail project identity and official distribution channels;
- archival or transfer of the project.

The maintainer should explain material technical decisions in an issue, pull
request, release note, or architecture decision record. Confidential security
and conduct matters remain private.

## Contributions and decisions

Contributions require DCO 1.1 sign-off and review under
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). There is no CLA and no contributor vote.
Consensus is preferred for ordinary design choices, but the maintainer resolves
deadlocks and may reject changes that weaken safety boundaries, add unsupported
operational commitments, or exceed the project's maintenance capacity.

## Moderation and conflicts

The maintainer enforces [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). If the
maintainer is personally involved in a conduct report, the reporter may request
an independent reviewer through the private contact. The project will identify
that reviewer before sharing confidential details.

## Succession and archival

New maintainers are appointed through a public repository change naming their
scope and authority. If active maintenance cannot continue, the maintainer may
appoint a successor, transfer the repository, or archive it. Archival will be
announced in the README and repository settings; it will not be presented as an
actively supported release.
